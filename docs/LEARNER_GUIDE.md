# Learner guide

1. Open <http://127.0.0.1:5001/learn>. The Academy lists 32 labs by domain.
2. Open a lab. Each has a **Learn** tab, a **Practice** tab (predict, then reveal a recorded run) and a **Prove** tab (sources, hashes, limitations).
3. Start with **LAB-HITL-APPROVAL**: on the Practice tab choose a prediction, reveal the recorded packet, and read the Prove tab. It is recorded and simulated; nothing runs live.
4. Investigate in Splunk when a lab points you at a search.
5. Then use the Centers:
   - **EVAL Center** (`/learn/evals`): read recorded experiments and their findings.
   - **Risk Center** (`/learn/risk`): write a risk assessment of your own from source-backed facts. It stays in your browser as a draft with status `LOCAL_DRAFT_NOT_APPROVED`.
   - **Governance Center** (`/learn/governance`): see which framework items relate to which controls, and where the gaps are.

A **Workshop path** bar on the lab, mission and Center pages links the six journey stages (Learn, Attack, Investigate, EVAL, Risk, Governance) in order and marks where you are. The Loan Desk mission has its own seven stages (Learn, Prepare, Attack, Investigate, Defend, Retest, Explain); its Attack stage is the only live one and unlocks after you lock a prediction on Prepare.

Every page labels its evidence as live, recorded, simulated or documented. A recorded run is one run, not a rate.
