# Privacy Evidence Ledger

| Claim | Evidence | Data Involved | Boundary | Confidence | Missing Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Classify each claim as:

- `OBSERVED`
- `INFERRED`
- `DOCUMENTED`
- `NOT OBSERVED`
- `NOT MODELED`
- `NOT PROVEN`

Use qualitative confidence with an explanation. Do not infer that ALLOW means
appropriate data use, that a preview is the complete record, or that missing
Splunk rows prove prevention.
