# Knowledge Check

Answer before opening Path B.

1. Which evidence establishes that a tool was requested?
2. Which evidence establishes the authorization decision, and which component made it?
3. Does `ALLOW` establish invocation or completion? Why?
4. What evidence supports invocation begin? What additional evidence supports completion?
5. What does a matching content hash establish? What does it not establish?
6. Why is a missing `mcp.started` event insufficient by itself to prove prevention?
7. What remained equivalent between ATTACK and RETEST? What changed?
8. Why can a normal `lookup_policy` execution match a broad tool-execution hunt without being malicious?
9. How may external Cisco or garak evidence inform a hypothesis without becoming authorization evidence?
10. Which incident claims are FACT, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, and UNKNOWN?
11. What evidence gap prevents a claim about authentication?
12. Write one technical and one executive conclusion without overstating severity.

## Review standard

A defensible answer cites a run ID, event/field, control, or repository artifact; separates request, authorization, invocation, completion, and outcome; names uncertainty; and states that Splunk is downstream evidence.
