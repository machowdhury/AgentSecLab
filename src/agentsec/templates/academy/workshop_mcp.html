    AcmeBank runs an AI assistant that helps staff answer policy questions. Its agent, {{ policy.agent_id }}, is granted one tool: {{ policy.allowed_tools }} with scope {{ policy.allowed_scopes }}.     Step 3 of 9 Predict: commit before you look

    What you are doing

    Why it matters
    A prediction made before seeing evidence shows you where your mental model is right or wrong.
    What to do
    Answer both questions, then lock your prediction.
    Evidence to inspect

      Step 6 of 9 Defend: what changes for the retest

      What you are doing
      Understanding the one configuration change between ATTACK and RETEST.
      Why it matters
      A fair retest changes one thing and keeps the request the same, so a difference in outcome can be tied to that change.
      What to do
      Read the comparison of the two configurations.
      Evidence to inspect
      The server-owned experiment definitions below.
      What you will learn
      Where enforcement lives: in the runtime control, not in this page and not in Splunk.

      Where the defence lives

      In the vulnerable profile, CTRL-MCP-001 is deliberately configured to fail open for a known but ungranted tool. The defended profile enforces the grant list. You do not change the policy here: the server owns it, and this page cannot authorize or block anything.
